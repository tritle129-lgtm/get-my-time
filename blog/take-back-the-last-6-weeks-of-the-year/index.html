Take back the last 6 weeks of the year

15 Nov 2026 · 3 min read

Topic: Daily habits Family and friends

Yes, you can. Six weeks is enough to change one phone habit. Pick one app, add a pause and a limit, and see what you get back.

Six weeks is 42 days

It is mid-November. The year ends in about six weeks. That is 42 days.

Say you scroll one hour a day. That is 42 hours. Almost two full days.

Those hours are yours. You can spend them on people you love.

Where did my evening go? Many of us ask that every December. The question is fair. The answer can change.

A night you may know

It is Tuesday night. Dinner is done. You sit on the sofa with your phone. Just a quick look, you think.

A video plays. Then another. The dishes wait. Someone asks about your day. You say mm and keep looking.

Then it is late. You are tired, and the evening is gone. Nothing bad happened. Time just slipped away.

This is not a flaw in you. It is how feeds are made.

Why the scroll pulls

A feed has no end. There is always one more video. Nothing tells you that you are done.

A book has a last page. A movie has end credits. A feed has neither.

Think of a tap. Water runs until you turn it off. A pause and a limit are your hand on the tap.

So you may not need more willpower. You may need a finish line. You can add your own.

What to do with the hours you get back

Free time needs a plan. If not, the feed takes it again. Here are ideas for the year-end weeks.

Pick two, not six. Two things you do beat six you skip.

  • Cook one meal with someone. Leave phones in another room.
  • Call a friend or a relative you miss.
  • Take a short walk after dinner.
  • Read ten pages of a book each night.
  • Write a card by hand for someone.
  • Plan one small outing for the holidays.

A tiny check-in each Sunday

Once a week, take one minute. Ask yourself three things.

  • Which app took the most of my time?
  • Did I get an evening back?
  • What did I do with it?

Common questions

Is six weeks enough to change a phone habit?

Yes, for one habit. Start with one app and one small limit. Small steps you can keep beat big plans you drop.

What should I do when I hit my limit?

Do the thing you picked: dinner, a book, a call. A limit works best when there is something you want to do instead.
