An app blocker for Android and iPhone

Get My Time is an app for Android and iPhone that limits the apps you pick, and with Golden Sand it can also block an app. It starts with a calm 10-second pause, not a hard lock.

Limit or block: what is the difference?

A limit gives you a daily amount of time on an app. When it is used, the app stops. A block shuts an app for hours, or until you turn it off.

A limit is gentler, and many people find it easier to keep. A block is firmer for times you want no feed at all.

What to look for in any app blocker

Before you pick one, check a few things.

  • Does it work on your phone, Android or iPhone?
  • Can you set it up in a few minutes?
  • Does it ask you to create an account?
  • Where does your data go?
  • Is it kind, or does it shame you?

How Get My Time does it

GMT is the same app on both phones. Pick your apps and set a daily limit. A 10-second pause comes before a limited app opens. At your limit, Time is up stops the app until midnight.

It also shows your time as money and as days of your year. No account is needed and your data stays on your phone.

Android and iPhone ask you to allow a few things first. That is how each phone works, and GMT walks you through it.

Free and paid

Sand is free and limits 2 apps. Golden Sand limits 10 apps, and adds Block an app, a Break reminder and all your history.

Common questions

Is there an app blocker for both Android and iPhone?

Yes. Get My Time is the same app on both, with the same screens, words and plans.

Can I really block an app with Get My Time?

Yes, with Golden Sand. You can block an app for hours, until you turn it off, or on a schedule.

Can I just turn it off when I want to scroll?

You are in charge, so yes, you can change your own limits. The pause and the cost are there to give you a moment to choose.

Is Get My Time a screen time app for kids?

No. It is for adults who want to scroll less on their own phone. It is not parental control.
