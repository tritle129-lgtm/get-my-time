How to use your phone less

To use your phone less, start with one app and add one small stop to it. Small, steady changes last longer than big rules.

Start with your why

Ask what you want the time for. A book. Dinner with family. A walk. A plan you keep putting off.

A clear why makes the change feel good, not like a punishment.

Small steps that work for many people

You do not need to quit your phone. Try one of these.

  • Delete the app you open without thinking.
  • Move feed apps off the first screen.
  • Turn off all notifications you do not need.
  • Leave the phone in another room at meals.
  • Try a grayscale screen for a week.
  • Do one thing without the phone each day.

Common questions

How can I use my phone less without deleting everything?

Pick the one or two apps that take the most time. Put a limit or a pause on those, and keep the rest.

How much phone use is too much?

There is no single number. A good question is whether the time went to things you care about.

Will I miss out if I use my phone less?

Calls, messages and maps still work. A limit only covers the apps you choose.

Does Get My Time lecture me?

No. It gives you a moment and a number, and you decide. HG, the plush hourglass, is a gentle friend, not a coach.
