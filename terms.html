Last updated: 31 October 2026

Short version: Get My Time ("GMT") is free to use. If you want more, you can get Golden Sand. Monthly and Yearly renew until you cancel; you can cancel any time in the store. "Get 10 more min" never charges you money. Your data stays on your phone.

1. What GMT is

GMT is a helper. It helps you spend less time on the apps you choose, and shows what that time is worth. We cannot promise any result: how much time you get back is up to you. You stay in charge of your phone. You can change a limit, turn off GMT or delete it whenever you want.

2. Free and Golden Sand

Free has no time limit. It includes:

  • A daily limit for up to 3 apps
  • The 10-second pause before an app opens, and the Time is up screen
  • The cost of your time in money and days of your year
  • My progress, reasons on the pause and the share card

Golden Sand adds, on top of Free:

  • Break reminder (a nudge after some minutes in one go)
  • Save your numbers as a spreadsheet (CSV) or a PDF report (a one-page summary)
  • More apps: up to 10

Golden Sand does not give extra minutes and does not skip the pause. If you already limit more than 3 apps before Golden Sand, you keep them.

There are three plans:

  • Monthly: renews every month until you cancel.
  • Yearly: renews every year until you cancel.
  • Forever: one payment. No renewal.

The price is the one Google Play or the App Store shows you. You always see the exact price and renewal time in the store's own screen before you pay. There is no free trial.

Payment is taken by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iPhone) from your store account. GMT does not see or keep your card details. GMT has no account of its own: your store account is the key to Golden Sand.

3. Cancel and refunds

  • Cancel any time: on Android in Google Play > Subscriptions; on iPhone in Settings > your name (Apple ID) > Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date, or the next period is charged.
  • You keep Golden Sand until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Google or Apple, under their rules. GMT cannot give refunds itself, because the store took the payment.
  • New phone? Open GMT > Settings > Restore purchases. Golden Sand comes back if you use the same Google or Apple account.

4. "Get 10 more min"

When you tap "Get 10 more min" on the Time is up screen, GMT gives you 10 more minutes and adds the price of that time (for example ฿5) to your own cost count, so you can see it. This is a mirror, not a bill. GMT never charges you real money for it.

5. Your data

Your usage data stays on your phone. GMT has no account, no ads and no server. Read the details in the Privacy policy.
