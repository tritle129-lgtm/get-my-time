How to stop scrolling at night

To stop scrolling at night, pick a stop time and make the feed harder to reach in bed. A small habit change does most of the work.

Why night scrolling happens

You are tired, and thinking is hard. The feed asks nothing of you. So you keep going, even when you want to stop.

It is a very common thing. You can set up your evening so it is easier.

Easy habits, no app needed

Try one or two of these this week.

  • Pick a time to put the phone down. Set an alarm for it.
  • Charge the phone across the room, not by your pillow.
  • Buy a cheap alarm clock, so you do not need the phone.
  • Keep a book where the phone used to be.
  • Turn off feed notifications after dinner.

How Get My Time helps in the evening

GMT is an app for Android and iPhone. Set a daily limit on your feed apps. When you reach it, Time is up stops the app until midnight. Close the app. Go to bed.

Before a limited app opens, you get a 10-second pause. It is a small moment to ask: do I want this now?

With Golden Sand, you can also block an app for chosen hours, such as late at night.

Common questions

How do I stop scrolling in bed?

Charge your phone away from the bed and set a time to put it down. Keep a book or an alarm clock near the bed instead.

Why do I keep scrolling when I am tired?

A feed needs no effort, so it is easy to keep going when you are tired. Making it harder to open helps.

Can Get My Time stop apps at a set hour at night?

Its daily limit stops an app when your time is used, until midnight. With Golden Sand you can also block an app for chosen hours.

Does the app work while I sleep?

It runs on your phone with no account. Time is up holds the app closed until midnight, then it opens again.
