Common questions

  • GMT says Screen Time is off (iPhone). Tap "Turn on again" in GMT and choose Allow. You can also check Settings > Screen Time.
  • Golden Sand on a new phone? Open GMT > Settings > Restore purchases, with the same Apple or Google account.
  • How do I cancel Golden Sand? iPhone: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Golden Sand. Android: Google Play > Subscriptions.
  • An app shows Time is up and I need it. Tap "10 more min". It adds 10 minutes and their value to your own cost; no money is taken. The app opens again after midnight.
  • Where is my data? On your phone only. GMT has no account and no server.
