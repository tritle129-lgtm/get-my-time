An app to limit TikTok, Instagram and YouTube

Yes, Get My Time is an app that limits the time you spend on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook and other apps you pick. You set a daily limit, and the app stops at that limit.

Limits without any app

You can start today, for free. These small steps help many people.

  • Put the app in a folder on the last page.
  • Turn off its notifications.
  • Log out after each use.
  • Use your phone's own time limit setting, if it has one.
  • Pick one app first. Do not fight them all at once.

How Get My Time limits an app

GMT is for Android and iPhone. You pick the apps, then set a daily limit with a simple wheel.

Each time you open a limited app, you get a 10-second pause first. You breathe, then you choose. Some days you will leave.

When the time is up, a Time is up screen stops the app. It opens again after midnight.

See what it costs you

GMT shows your time on each app as money and as days of your year. One hour a day is more than 15 days a year. A number like that is harder to ignore than minutes.

What is free

Sand is free. It limits 2 apps, up to 2 hours a day each. Golden Sand limits 10 apps, up to 4 hours a day each, and adds more help. No account is needed.

In one sentence

Get My Time (GMT) is a free-to-start app for Android and iPhone that helps you scroll less: it adds a 10-second pause before a limited app opens, shows your screen time as money and days of your year, and stops the app at your daily limit until midnight. No account is needed and your data stays on your phone.

Common questions

Can I limit TikTok and Instagram at the same time?

Yes. Free Sand covers 2 apps, so you can pick two. Golden Sand covers 10.

What happens when I hit my limit on an app?

A Time is up screen appears and the app stays closed until midnight. If you set a limit below today's use, Time is up shows at once.

Does it work on iPhone as well as Android?

Yes. On iPhone you choose apps with Apple's picker, and the pause is a shield where you pour the sand for 10 seconds.

Can I block an app completely?

Yes, with Golden Sand. You can block an app for chosen hours, or until you turn the block off.
