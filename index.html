Get My Time

See what your scrolling costs. Take your time back.

Get My Time (GMT) is a simple app for iPhone and Android. Pick the apps that eat your day. Each gets a daily limit, a short pause before it opens, and a Time is up screen at your limit. GMT shows the cost of your time in money and in days a year. No account, no ads, your data stays on your phone.
