In one sentence

Get My Time (GMT) is a free-to-start app for Android and iPhone that helps you scroll less: it adds a 10-second pause before a limited app opens, shows your screen time as money and days of your year, and stops the app at your daily limit until midnight. No account is needed and your data stays on your phone.

Common questions

Is Get My Time free?

It is free to start. Sand (free) lets you limit 2 apps, with the pause, Time is up and the cost of your time. Golden Sand is a paid upgrade with more help.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no login. You open the app and set your first limit.

Does it work on Android and iPhone?

Yes. It is the same app on both phones, with the same screens and words. A few steps differ only where Android or Apple require it.

Is the app in Thai?

Not yet. The app screens are English only for now, and this website is in Thai and English.

Is it parental control?

No. GMT is for adults who want to scroll less on their own phone. It is not made to control another person's phone.

How is it different from my phone's own tools?

Those tools show minutes on a chart. GMT also pauses you before the app opens, shows what the time costs in money and days, and stops the app at your limit.

What happens when I reach my limit?

A Time is up screen appears and the app stays closed until midnight. After midnight it opens again.

Where is my data?

On your phone. GMT needs no account, and it sends nothing out.
