How to stop doom scrolling

To stop doom scrolling, add a small pause before you open the feed and set a clear end to your time. You do not need strong willpower. You need a gentle stop in the way.

Why it is hard to stop

Feeds never end. There is always one more video. Nothing tells you when you are done.

So it is not a flaw in you. The feed has no finish line. You can add one.

Things anyone can do, with no app

Start with these. They are free and they work for many people.

  • Move the feed apps off your home screen.
  • Turn off notifications from the feed.
  • Log out after each use, so opening takes a step.
  • Pick a stop time and set an alarm.
  • Charge your phone away from your bed.
  • Decide what you will do instead: a book, a walk, a call.

One more way: Get My Time

GMT is an app for Android and iPhone. It adds a 10-second pause before a limited app opens. You breathe, then you choose.

It shows what your time costs, in money and in days of your year. One hour a day is more than 15 days a year.

Common questions

Is doom scrolling my fault?

No. Feeds are made to keep going, with no end point. Adding your own stop is a smart move, not a sign of weakness.

What is the first step to stop doom scrolling?

Pick the one app you scroll most and add a small stop to it. A pause, a limit, or moving it off your home screen all count.

Can an app help me stop doom scrolling?

It can add a pause and a stop. You still choose. GMT gives you a 10-second pause before a limited app opens and stops it at your daily limit.

Does Get My Time need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, and your data stays on your phone.
