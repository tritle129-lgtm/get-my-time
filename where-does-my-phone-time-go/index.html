Where does my phone time go?

The fastest way to learn where your phone time goes is to look at one full day, app by app. Most of it goes into just two or three apps.

Look first, change later

You do not need a plan yet. First, just look. Here is an easy way.

  • Guess your daily phone time before you check. Write it down.
  • Open your phone's settings and find its usage list.
  • Look at one full day. Note the top three apps.
  • Ask one question for each: would I miss it if it was gone?
  • Check again in a week. Is the top three the same?

Minutes are easy to ignore

A chart of minutes is a fine start. But 94 minutes is just a number. It does not feel like anything.

Time feels bigger when you see what it is worth. One hour a day is more than 15 days a year.

Common questions

How can I see how much time I spend on my phone?

Open your phone's settings and look for its usage list. Check one full day, not just a few hours.

Why does my phone time feel so hard to track?

Short checks add up without you noticing. Ten quick opens can be an hour by the end of the day.

How does Get My Time show what my time is worth?

It uses a price for one hour of your time. You can change that price and the currency. It then shows your phone time as money and as days of your year.

Does Get My Time send my usage anywhere?

No. Your data stays on your phone, and there is no account.
