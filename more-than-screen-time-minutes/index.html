Is there something better than the phone's own screen time tools?

Your phone's own tools are good at one thing: they show how many minutes you spent. Get My Time adds three more things: a pause, the cost of your time, and a stop.

What the phone's own tools do well

They are built in and free. They show minutes on a chart, per app. For many people, that is a useful start.

If a chart is all you need, use it. There is no reason to add an app.

Where minutes fall short

Many people see the chart, feel a little bad, and keep scrolling. Minutes are small and easy to ignore.

The chart also does not stand between you and the next open. It tells you after.

What Get My Time adds

GMT works on Android and iPhone. It adds three things, in this order:

  • A 10-second pause before a limited app opens. You breathe, then you choose.
  • Your time shown as money and as days of your year. One hour a day is more than 15 days a year.
  • Time is up at your daily limit. The app opens again after midnight.

Use both, if you like

You do not have to pick one. Look at your minutes there, and let GMT do the pause and the stop. GMT stays kind. It does not shame you.

Free to start. No account. Your data stays on your phone.

In one sentence

Get My Time (GMT) is a free-to-start app for Android and iPhone that helps you scroll less: it adds a 10-second pause before a limited app opens, shows your screen time as money and days of your year, and stops the app at your daily limit until midnight. No account is needed and your data stays on your phone.

Common questions

Why use an app when my phone already shows my screen time?

A chart shows minutes after the fact. GMT acts before the app opens, with a pause, and at your limit, with a stop.

What does Get My Time show that minutes do not?

It shows your time as money and as days of your year. That makes the time feel real.

Do I need to stop using my phone's built-in tools?

No. Keep them if they help. GMT works on its own and does not need them.

Is Get My Time free?

It is free to start, with Sand (free) for 2 apps. Golden Sand is an upgrade with more help. No account is needed.
